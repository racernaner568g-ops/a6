TABLE STUDIO / METHOD

A setting is a choreography of reach, light and time.

Our editorial method connects guest count, table size, menu, materials, lighting and clearing into one understandable plan.

01

Count

Define seats, table dimensions and comfortable place width.

02

Ground

Choose linen or a bare surface that supports the meal.

03

Compose

Arrange only the vessels, light and serving pieces required.

04

Rehearse

Test reach, sightlines, hot dishes and the clearing route.

SERVICE RHYTHM / 05

The best plan leaves room to be present.

Prepare what can be finished early, decide where used dishes will go, and keep one open surface for the next course. Structure should reduce interruption rather than make the host perform.
